Privacy policy

Shopkeep is made by Team Coge. Last updated 7 November 2026.

Shopkeep is a sales assistant that shops connect to their Instagram professional account. It answers customers' direct messages, takes orders and sends payment links on the shop's behalf. This page explains what data Shopkeep handles and why.

What we collect

  • Shop owners: name, email, a hashed password, shop details and products you enter, your Instagram username and an access token Instagram gives us when you connect, and your Razorpay keys if you add them (stored encrypted).
  • Customers who message a shop: the messages they send and receive in that conversation, their Instagram name and username, and the order details they give (name, phone number, delivery address, what they ordered, payment status).

How we use it

  • To reply to customers' messages for the shop, using an AI model (Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude) that receives the conversation and the shop's catalog to write each reply.
  • To create orders and payment links (through the shop's own Razorpay account) and show them to the shop in its dashboard.
  • We don't sell data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the services needed to run Shopkeep: our hosting and database providers, the AI provider, Instagram (Meta) and the shop's payment provider.

Who can see it

Each shop sees only its own conversations, customers and orders. Customer data belongs to the shop the customer messaged.

How long we keep it

As long as the shop's account is active. When a shop disconnects Instagram, we delete its access token immediately. When a shop closes its account, we delete its data within 30 days.

Deleting your data

Customers: ask the shop you messaged, or email us at the address below with your Instagram username and the shop's name, and we'll delete your messages and personal details within 30 days. Shops: email us from your account email to close your account and delete all its data.
