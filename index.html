Questions

Does it pretend to be a person?

No. It talks like your staff, but if anyone asks, it says it's your shop's after-hours assistant and that your team is back at 9 AM.

Is my Instagram account safe?

Shopkeep connects through Meta's official Instagram Messaging API, the same way large brands do. You log in with Meta; we never see your password.

What if it doesn't know the answer?

It says so, flags the chat for you, and you get a WhatsApp alert. It never invents prices or products.

Which languages does it speak?

English and Tamil written in English letters (Tanglish) out of the box. More can be added.

How do payments work?

UPI payment links through your own payment-gateway account. Money goes straight to you; paid orders confirm automatically.
