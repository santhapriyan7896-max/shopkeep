## Recording your own voice over the music-only version

1. Record each line on your phone (quiet room, phone about 20 cm away). One take for the whole script is fine.
2. Copy the file into this `video` folder and tell Claude. It'll place each line on its scene and duck the music under your voice, the same way the robot draft is mixed.
3. Or do it yourself in Shotcut: open `activewear33-pitch-music-only.mp4`, add your recording on a new audio track, and lower the video's own audio to about −6 dB while you speak.
